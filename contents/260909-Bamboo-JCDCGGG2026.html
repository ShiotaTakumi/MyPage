Upper Bounds on the Backlog of a Greedy Strategy
for Bamboo Garden Trimming

Akitoshi Kawamura  ·  Hirotaka Ono  ·  Takumi Shiota
JCDCG³ 2026

In Bamboo Garden Trimming, each bamboo grows at its own constant growth rate per day, and we cut exactly one of them each day to keep the garden low. The simplest strategy is the greedy one that always cuts the tallest bamboo, known as Reduce-Max.

The maximum height ever reached is called the backlog. We show that for at most four bamboos the backlog stays below twice the total growth rate, while in general there are instances that exceed twice the total growth rate.

This page collects supplementary material to help follow those constructions.
