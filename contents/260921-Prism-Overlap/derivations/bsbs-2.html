3. Testing the remaining pairs3. 残った組の判定

        
Two segments cross when the two ends of each lie strictly on opposite sides of the line through the other. On which side a point lies is given by the sign of its signed distance from the line, which is a cross product with a unit vector along the line. For the lines through $v_0$ and $v_1$ and through $v_1$ and $v_2$, whose unit normals $(\cos\alpha, \sin\alpha)$ and $(\cos 2\alpha, \sin 2\alpha)$ point away from $\mathsf{B}_1$, let $f_0(p) = p \cdot (\cos\alpha, \sin\alpha)$ and $f_1(p) = (p - v_1) \cdot (\cos 2\alpha, \sin 2\alpha)$; $\mathsf{B}_1$ lies in $f_0 \le 0$ and in $f_1 \le 0$, as $f_0(v_{n-1}) = f_1(v_0) = -\sin\alpha$. For the corners of $\mathsf{S}_2$, the product-to-sum formulas give
